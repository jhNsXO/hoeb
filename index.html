Your words, perfected.

From the first draft to the final layout, I help authors and academics transform their manuscripts into publication-ready books.

As an independent editor, I offer precise editing and proofreading in both English and German, professional typesetting, and expert index creation. By pairing traditional editorial excellence with cutting-edge editorial software and tools, I ensure your text is sharp, accurate, and beautifully presented.

Precise formatting and typesetting of scientific articles and books according to required publisher guidelines and standards.
